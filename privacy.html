Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Phase17 collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Phase17 is the trading name of PHASE17 LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17488592). We are the controller of the personal information described here.

Contact for privacy questions: hello@phaseseventeen.com

What we collect

  • When you book a call: your name, email address, and anything you choose to tell us in the booking form, such as your company and what you'd like to discuss.
  • When you contact us: your name, contact details and the content of your message, by email or LinkedIn.
  • When we work together: business contact details for the people involved, and notes from interviews and workshops that form part of the engagement.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To arrange and hold the call you asked for, and to reply to you. Basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract.
  • To deliver work you've engaged us for, and to invoice for it. Basis: performance of a contract, and our legal obligations for accounting and tax.
  • To follow up on a conversation you started with us. Basis: our legitimate interest in running the business. We won't add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of providers who process information on our behalf:

  • Zoho (EU data centre) for email, calendar and call booking.
  • GitHub Pages to host this website, which records basic technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs.
  • Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser loads this site's typefaces.

Some of these providers may transfer information outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and call notes that don't lead to work: up to 2 years after our last contact.
  • Client and invoicing records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax rules require.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. Contact us at the address above and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how we use personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
