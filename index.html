Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

We already have Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude. What exactly would you do?

We start with the work, not the tool. We look at how your existing AI tools are being used, speak to the people doing the work and identify the workflows where changing the way the work happens could create the most value.

The Diagnostic turns that into a prioritised set of opportunities, ownership and an implementation roadmap.

Does IT need to be involved?

Usually, yes, but the level of involvement depends on the workflow and the tools already in place.

Phase17 works primarily with the business teams that own the work, while involving the relevant technology or IT people where access, security, governance, integrations or technical decisions are involved.

AI implementation shouldn’t be an IT-only project, and it shouldn’t happen without the right technical oversight.

Do we need to change our existing AI tools?

Usually not. Phase17 starts with the AI tools you already pay for and looks at how to get more operational value from them.

If a valuable workflow cannot be supported by the tools already in place, that constraint should be made explicit rather than defaulting to another technology purchase.

What kinds of workflows are suitable?

The best candidates are repeatable workflows where people spend meaningful time finding information, producing or reviewing content, moving information between systems, making routine decisions or completing manual steps.

We prioritise workflows where changing the way the work happens could produce a measurable operational improvement. The Diagnostic is designed to identify which of those opportunities are actually worth pursuing in your business.

How do you measure whether the work has delivered value?

Success measures are agreed at the start and depend on the workflow being changed.

The aim is to establish a credible before-and-after so you can see whether the new way of working has produced a measurable operational improvement.

What happens after the Diagnostic?

You leave with a prioritised roadmap you can implement with Phase17 or without us.

If you want hands-on support, Embedded Implementation turns the priority workflows into working changes inside the business over 6–8 weeks.

Can we take the Diagnostic roadmap and implement it ourselves?

Yes. The Diagnostic is designed to be useful as a standalone engagement.

You leave with the priorities, value and effort assessment, recommended ownership and implementation roadmap needed to take the work forward internally if that is the right option for you.

What does the Diagnostic cost?

The AI Implementation Diagnostic takes two weeks and is fixed price from £3,500.

How much time will you need from our team?

We keep the demand on your team focused. We’ll need access to the people who understand the workflows we’re assessing, plus someone who can make decisions and own the outcome.

Before the Diagnostic starts, we’ll agree who needs to be involved and when, so there are no surprises or unnecessary meetings.

Do you build custom AI systems?

That is not the core Phase17 offer.

Phase17 focuses on helping businesses get measurable value from the AI tools they already own through workflow redesign, implementation, adoption and measurement. If you are primarily looking for a custom AI product to be built from scratch, Phase17 is probably not the right fit.
