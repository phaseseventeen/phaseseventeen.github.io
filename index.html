Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

How much does an AI Implementation Diagnostic cost?

The Diagnostic is a fixed-price, two-week engagement starting from £3,500. The final price is agreed in writing before any work starts. Embedded Implementation is scoped and priced after the Diagnostic, once we know which workflows are worth changing.

How long does it take?

The Diagnostic takes two weeks. Embedded Implementation runs for six to eight weeks.

What do we get at the end of the Diagnostic?

You get a readiness view across people, process, data and governance; three to five AI opportunities ranked by value, feasibility and risk; a baseline and business case for the top three; and a roadmap your team can act on with or without us.

Do we need to buy new AI tools?

Usually not. We start with the tools you already pay for, such as Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude, Gemini or Agentforce. Where something else is needed, we say whether to buy, partner or use simpler automation.

Who is Phase17 for?

Leaders who own change at UK B2B companies with 50–500 staff: usually the MD or CEO, the COO or Operations Director, working with the CTO or Head of IT. You've invested in AI and need it to show up in the numbers. We also work with Customer Success leaders in SaaS businesses.

What happens on the 20-minute call?
